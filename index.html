ABOUT US

Since 2005, Alpha Food Service has played an important role in shaping Armenia’s culinary culture by importing premium bakery and confectionery ingredients, professional food products, and a wide range of beverages from the World’s leading brands.

Today, the company partners with nearly 3,000 businesses across the HoReCa, bakery and retail sectors in Armenia, supplying them with a wide portfolio of high-quality products that meet the highest international standards.

Over the years, Alpha Food Service has built not only a reliable and efficient supply chain, but has also become a driving force in the development of the industry.

MISSION

Our mission is to improve the quality of life of people in Armenia and bring more diversity into it, enriching it with new experiences and tastes of the world’s eno-gastronomic culture.

VISION

We envision to develop the culture of preparing and consuming bakery and confectionery products, food, as well as non-alcoholic and alcoholic beverages both in the professional and home segments, up to the highest international levels.

OUR TEAM

Our team is made up of dedicated professionals who share a passion for quality, innovation, and excellent service.

Through teamwork, experience, and a strong sense of responsibility, we strive to create value for our partners, customers, and the communities we work with.

Simultaneously, we invest in the development of our people, recognizing that expertise and adaptability are key drivers of long-term success. SHOPS

Since 2019, Alpha Food Service has entered the retail market with its own brands — The House and The Bake House. The goal has been simple: to enhance and elevate consumer food culture, enabling individuals to access and experience professional-grade culinary products within their own kitchens.

In 2019, the first The House store opened, setting a new benchmark in client consultation and service. Today, three The House stores operate, specializing in fine wines, premium spirits & gourmet food.

Website Instagram

In 2023, The Bake House store was launched, offering a wide selection of premium baking ingredients alongside essential tools for both professional confectioners and home baking enthusiasts.

Studios

As part of its commitment to advancing the industry, Alpha Food Service supports the growth of professional communities through regular educational programs, master classes, tastings, and training sessions, held both on its own platforms and internationally.


Alpha Food Service’s knowledge platforms include The House and The Bake House Studios — technically advanced and fully equipped, where leading local and international experts conduct a wide range of events, providing industry professionals with the opportunity to explore the latest innovations and global trends.

FACILITIES

On a land plot with a total area of 24,000 sq. m, Alpha Food Service is currently developing a new office and logistics complex, with commissioning planned for autumn 2026. The new complex includes a modern studio space designed for up to 100 participants, and more than 9,000 sq.m of warehouse capacity equipped with different temperature regimes.
